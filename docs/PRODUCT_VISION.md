# Product Vision

## Project

**Product name:** TradieOS  
**AI assistant name:** Tori  
**Tagline:** Your AI Office Manager

## Executive summary

TradieOS helps Australian tradies run the office side of their business without needing to become admin experts. It is built for solo tradies, small teams, growing service businesses, and larger trade businesses that need better follow-up, quoting, invoicing, scheduling, and customer communication.

The core product belief is that tradies do not need another complicated job management system. They need a dependable AI office manager that helps them stay organised, get paid faster, communicate professionally, and keep their day under control.

## Mission

Help Australian tradies spend less time on admin and more time doing paid work by giving every business an AI office manager called Tori.

## Vision

TradieOS becomes the trusted operating system for small trade businesses across Australia, where Tori manages admin drafts, reminders, follow-ups, summaries, priorities, and business insights while the tradie stays in control of final actions.

## Goals

- Reduce admin workload for Australian tradies.
- Help businesses quote faster.
- Help businesses invoice and follow up consistently.
- Help owners understand today’s priorities quickly.
- Improve customer communication quality.
- Keep every business workspace securely isolated.
- Make AI practical, safe, and useful instead of gimmicky.

## Non-goals

- Do not become generic enterprise ERP software.
- Do not build broad job management features unless they support the AI office manager mission.
- Do not send SMS, email, quotes, or invoices without user confirmation.
- Do not provide legal, financial, medical, or compliance advice as a substitute for a professional.
- Do not expose or infer cross-business data.
- Do not build features that add complexity without saving time, making money, or reducing stress.

## Product philosophy

TradieOS is not job management software.

TradieOS is an AI Office Manager.

Every feature must answer:

1. Does this save time?
2. Does this make money?
3. Does this reduce stress?

If not, do not build it.

## Target customers

### Australian tradies

Trade and service businesses operating in Australia with local customer relationships, on-site work, quotes, invoices, scheduling, and customer follow-up needs.

### Solo tradies

Owners doing both field work and admin themselves. They need simple workflows, mobile-first usage, fast quoting, and reminders.

### Small teams

Businesses with a small crew, admin helper, or family member helping with office tasks. They need shared visibility, basic roles, and better follow-up.

### Growing businesses

Businesses adding staff, subcontractors, repeat customers, and more formal processes. They need structure, reports, and integrations.

### Large businesses

Larger trade businesses with office staff, dispatching, accounting workflows, and multiple roles. They need advanced permissions, audit history, and integrations.

## Positioning

Tori is not a chatbot.

Tori is an AI employee.

The product should feel like hiring a calm, reliable office manager who can draft, summarise, remind, prioritise, and recommend, while always asking before sending or changing anything important.
